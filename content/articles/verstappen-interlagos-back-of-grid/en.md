Max Verstappen started the São Paulo Grand Prix 21st and finished it second, 14.602 seconds behind Lando Norris. It was the third year in a row that he lined up near the back at Interlagos because of an engine penalty, and the third year in a row that he went home with a trophy.

## Three penalties, three podiums

In 2024 he qualified 12th and took a five-place penalty for using too many power unit parts. From 17th on the grid he won a wet race ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_S%C3%A3o_Paulo_Grand_Prix)). In 2025 Red Bull changed his power unit under parc fermé, so he started from the pit lane, recovered from an early puncture and finished third. This year the team fitted a new power unit before qualifying, which meant a 30-place drop and 21st on the grid ([ESPN](https://www.espn.com/f1/story/_/id/50389072/max-verstappen-sao-paulo-grand-prix-brazil-formula-1)). Red Bull saved his wet tyres for Sunday rather than running in Q2, as we described in our [qualifying report](/articles/sao-paulo-qualifying-chaos).

## Rain at both ends

The weather helped him again. Everyone started on intermediates, the track dried enough for slicks after the first stint, and rain came back near the end, so the field went back onto intermediates. Verstappen made a slow getaway, saying afterwards he had held the clutch wrong, but he was in the points by lap 3 and passed car after car through the first stint ([Pit Debrief](https://www.pitdebrief.com/post/verstappen-believes-more-was-possible-after-p21-to-p2-comeback-at-2026-f1-sao-paulo-gp/)).

Ahead of him the race kept changing. Antonelli and Liam Lawson both got past Norris off the line, then Antonelli spun at Turn 1 and later went straight on at the same corner, and Norris took control. Verstappen was voted driver of the day.

F1 summed up his afternoon in two short lines: started P21, finished P2. The photo shows him on the podium, the trophy raised in one hand and the other hand up to the crowd.

## "What a disaster to drive"

He was not celebrating. On slicks he had heavy oversteer in the fast corners, and after the flag he said: "I'm sorry, but what a disaster to drive." He believes he spent most of the race with a damaged car, and that there was more to come if it had been in good shape. Back on intermediates in the final laps, he said, the car felt night and day different. Red Bull now want to find out whether something broke ([ESPN](https://www.espn.com/f1/story/_/id/50389072/max-verstappen-sao-paulo-grand-prix-brazil-formula-1)).

## Eight podiums in a row

The bigger picture is his form. Second in Brazil was Verstappen's eighth podium in a row, from Monza onwards, with wins in Bahrain, Singapore and Mexico among them. That run is worth 159 points. Before Monza he had retired three times and finished 20th at Silverstone.

## Two Red Bulls on the podium

Isack Hadjar started fifth and finished third, his third podium of the year after Monaco and Azerbaijan and the fourth of his career, in 41 races. It was Red Bull's second double podium of 2026; the first was in Baku, where Verstappen was second and Hadjar third.

## Second place is his to lose

Verstappen now has 279 points and is second in the championship, 17 ahead of George Russell and 18 ahead of Lewis Hamilton. Three races are left, with 75 points for a driver who wins them all. Next up is [Las Vegas](/calendar/2026/21), a night race in the desert, where rain is rare.
