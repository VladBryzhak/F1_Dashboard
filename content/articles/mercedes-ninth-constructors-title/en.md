Mercedes are constructors' champions again. In a wet São Paulo Grand Prix, George Russell finished fourth and Kimi Antonelli fifth, and that was enough: with three races left, nobody can catch them. It is the team's ninth constructors' title and its first since 2021.

## 142 points with 129 left

Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi are all normal weekends, with no sprints. A team can score at most 43 points per race, a win and a second place, so 129 points are still on the table. Mercedes lead Ferrari by 142. Ferrari have 510 points, Red Bull 415 and McLaren 398. The race in Brazil did not even need to go well: Lando Norris won it for McLaren, Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar put both Red Bulls on the podium, and Mercedes still closed it out.

## Built in the first six races

The title was won in spring. Mercedes won the first six races of the year: Russell in Australia, then Antonelli in China, Japan, Miami, Canada and Monaco. Three of the team's 12 wins were one-twos: Australia, China and Italy. Antonelli took nine of the wins and the drivers' title in Mexico, Russell three, in Australia, Austria and Azerbaijan.

Just as telling is what never happened. Mercedes scored in all 20 races. Their worst weekend was Singapore, with 17 points. Ferrari's two wins this year came from Hamilton in Barcelona and Leclerc at Silverstone, and both times Mercedes still had a car on the podium.

## Two rule changes, two titles

The last time F1 brought in a new generation of power units was 2014, with the first hybrid V6 engines. Mercedes won that year and every year until 2021: eight constructors' titles in a row, a record. The 2022 rules, built around ground effect, did not suit them. In four seasons they finished third, second, fourth and second, with seven wins in total.

The 2026 rules brought new power units, with a much larger share of electric power, and smaller, lighter cars. Mercedes got it right from the first race, as they did twelve years ago. Antonelli's season is covered in [The Champion's season in numbers](/articles/antonelli-champion-season-in-numbers); this title belongs to the whole team, with Russell's three wins and 262 points in it.

## Level with Williams

With nine titles, Mercedes draw level with Williams. Only two teams have more: McLaren with 10 and Ferrari with 16. Every one of the nine has come since 2014, in the team's current form. Mercedes the works team returned to F1 in 2010, after Brawn's title season of 2009.

## What is left

The constructors' title is decided, and so is the drivers'. The fight still open is for second place among the drivers: Verstappen 279, Russell 262, Hamilton 261, with three races left. Russell can still give Mercedes a one-two in the final table.
