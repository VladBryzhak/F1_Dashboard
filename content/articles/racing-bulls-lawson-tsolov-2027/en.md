Before São Paulo, Liam Lawson said there was "definitely no assurance" he would keep his seat. On Thursday Racing Bulls ended the wait: Lawson and Arvid Lindblad will drive for the team again in 2027 ([Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/lawson-and-lindblad-to-remain-at-racing-bulls-for-2027-as-team-confirm-unchanged-line-up.1vwKafZKAx8zw2Tb4B8fPx)). Nikola Tsolov, the Red Bull junior who was pushing for that seat, becomes the official reserve driver of both Red Bull teams instead.

"I have no doubt that this is the best driver line-up for us," said team boss Alan Permane.

Formula 1 marked the news with a short video on Instagram. It opens on a close-up portrait in blue under the words "2027 line-up" and the Racing Bulls name.

## Lawson earned it on track

[Liam Lawson](/drivers/liam-lawson) is ninth in the championship with 89 points, his best season by a distance. His previous best was 38 points and 14th place in 2025. The form has been strongest exactly when the decision was being made.

```stats
title: The last five Grands Prix
- Lawson: 7th, 7th, 9th, 7th, 7th
- Lawson's points: 26
- Lindblad's points: 1
- Racing Bulls in the constructors' table: 5th, 114 points
- Gap to Alpine in 6th: 45 points
source: Paddock Times (f1db), Bahrain to São Paulo
```

In São Paulo he started third and finished seventh, his fourth seventh place in five races. After the news he named fifth place in the constructors' table as the team's next target. With 45 points over Alpine and three rounds left, that fight is close to won.

## Lindblad: a rookie year to build on

[Arvid Lindblad](/drivers/arvid-lindblad) turned 19 in August and has 38 points in his first season, 11th in the standings. His recent run has been quieter: no points in the last four races, and a retirement in Mexico. Keeping him is a bet on a second season, which is what Racing Bulls exist for. Lindblad said he wants to build on what he learned as a rookie.

## Tsolov: a reserve with a plan

Tsolov has been the name in this story for months. He is second in Formula 2 with six wins, fighting Rafael Câmara, who joins Haas in 2027, and Alex Dunne for the title with the Qatar and Abu Dhabi rounds still to come ([The Race](https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/tsolov-gets-twin-f1-2027-reserve-role-to-prepare-him-for-race-seat/)). He made his F1 practice debut for Racing Bulls in Mexico.

Red Bull say the new role is more than sitting in the garage. It combines simulator and development work with free practice sessions, tests of older cars, selected test days and tyre testing. Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies said the focus in 2027 is to give him the experience and tools he needs. Tsolov was direct: "My ambition is clear: I want to become an F1 driver" ([RaceFans](https://www.racefans.net/2026/11/12/racing-bulls-keep-lawson-and-lindblad-as-tsolov-gets-red-bull-reserve-role/)).

The catch is that a reserve seat is not a race seat. Red Bull now have four contracted race drivers and one reserve they clearly want to promote. For Tsolov to race in 2028, one of the four has to make way.

## What it means for Cadillac

This was the decision Cadillac were waiting on. As we wrote in [our look at Bottas's seat](/articles/cadillac-bottas-seat-2027), Lawson was the strongest name that could have come onto the market. That option is now gone.

The second Cadillac is one of only two seats still open for 2027, along with Haas's second car, where Oliver Bearman is expected to stay. Formula1.com notes that Colton Herta now has his super licence, which leaves Cadillac choosing between Valtteri Bottas's experience and an American driver for an American team.
