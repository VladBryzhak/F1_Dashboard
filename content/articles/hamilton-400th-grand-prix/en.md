The São Paulo Grand Prix was Lewis Hamilton's 400th start in Formula 1. Only one driver has reached that number before him: Fernando Alonso, who now has 445. Hamilton marked it at Interlagos, the circuit where he became world champion for the first time, in the country that made him an honorary citizen in 2022.

## "I never thought that I would be reaching numbers like that"

Hamilton was asked about the milestone before the weekend. He called it "kind of crazy" and said the number itself meant little to him, but that it was beautiful to reach it in Brazil ([Reuters via TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/motoring/2026-11-06-hamilton-marks-400th-race-in-honorary-home-of-brazil/)). In Mexico he had set himself a goal: to win his 400th race.

Alonso welcomed him to the club. "Yes, of course. It's a big number," he said, and added that more drivers will join it with calendars this long ([Crash.net](https://www.crash.net/f1/news/1109337/1/fernando-alonso-reacts-lewis-hamilton-joins-his-exclusive-f1-club)).

## The race itself: from 20th to ninth

The day did not go to plan. A problem with his fresh Ferrari engine in final practice forced a full power unit change, and Hamilton started 20th, as we described in our [qualifying report](/articles/sao-paulo-qualifying-chaos). In a wet race he finished ninth, one lap down, and scored two points. The win went to Lando Norris, with Max Verstappen [second from 21st](/articles/verstappen-interlagos-back-of-grid).

## Three teams, one record book

Hamilton's 400 starts split across three teams: 110 with McLaren from 2007 to 2012, 246 with Mercedes from 2013 to 2024 and 44 with Ferrari since 2025. He won 21 races with McLaren, 84 with Mercedes and one with Ferrari, in Barcelona this June.

He started on the podium: third on his debut in Australia in 2007. One win in every four starts, roughly, and a podium in more than half of them: 209 from 400. His 106 wins and 105 poles are both records. The 105th pole came a week earlier, in Mexico.

## Interlagos, his second home

São Paulo has been part of Hamilton's story from the start. In 2008 he finished fifth there and took his first title by a single point. He has won at Interlagos three times: in 2016, in 2018 and in 2021, when he won from tenth on the grid. Including those wins, he has seven podiums at the circuit from 19 starts.

For the 400th he wore Brazil's colours: a yellow helmet with green stripes. Ferrari posted the weekend with a short line, "Milestone race 400 checked off in São Paulo". The photos show the helmet up close and his car in the garage under the number 44 board, surrounded by mechanics.

## What comes next

Hamilton is fourth in the championship with 261 points, 18 behind Verstappen in second, with three races left. He has said he is "still all in" at Ferrari for the foreseeable future, and he will race for the team in 2027 ([Crash.net](https://www.crash.net/f1/news/1109337/1/fernando-alonso-reacts-lewis-hamilton-joins-his-exclusive-f1-club)). Alonso, who is signed with Aston Martin for 2027, stays 45 starts ahead. Hamilton can only close that gap if Alonso stops first.
